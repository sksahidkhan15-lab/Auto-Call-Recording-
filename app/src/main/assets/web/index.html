Why doesn't any third-party app record direct cellular audio on modern Android?

Starting with Android 9 (Pie) and finalized in Android 10, Google removed the permission for third-party applications to tap into raw cellular call audio (VOICE_CALL). In 2022, Google further restricted the Accessibility API to prevent apps from covertly bypassing this security barrier.

Our Ethical & Supported Approach: We utilize Android's authorized AudioSource.VOICE_COMMUNICATION and AudioSource.MIC with telephony state monitoring. Turning on speakerphone during calls allows capturing both voices cleanly without exploiting system vulnerabilities or violating privacy laws.
